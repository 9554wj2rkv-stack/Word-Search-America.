# App Store screenshot plan: Word Search America

All screenshots were captured from the working game (`index.html`) after real, automated play. Nothing is mocked up: every number, badge and puzzle on screen was produced by the game itself. They were rendered in a desktop Chromium engine at iPhone sizes, so before submission, re-capture or spot-check them on a real iPhone (or the iOS Simulator) once the app is wrapped.

## Sizes (Apple App Store Connect, checked October 2026)

| Folder | Pixels (portrait) | Display group |
|---|---|---|
| `screenshots-6.9in/` | 1320 × 2868 | iPhone with Dynamic Island, large display (Pro Max models) |
| `screenshots-6.3in/` | 1206 × 2622 | iPhone with Dynamic Island, medium display |

Apple accepts PNG or JPEG with no transparency (these files have none), up to 10 per size. Smaller iPhone sizes are scaled down automatically from the largest set you upload.
