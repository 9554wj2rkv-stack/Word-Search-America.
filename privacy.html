Privacy Policy

Word Search America · North Spark 21
Last updated: 12 November 2026

Game data on your device

The game stores scores, completed puzzles, achievements, daily streaks and preferences locally on your device. This version does not send this game data to us and does not include accounts, advertising or analytics services.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell your game data or share it with advertisers. This version does not include tracking tools. If you play through a website, GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process connection information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure it; this policy describes the game itself.

Keeping and deleting data

Local progress remains until it is reset or the device’s stored app or browser data is cleared. Settings → Reset progress clears your game progress while keeping your sound and animation preferences. To remove all stored game data, clear this game’s browser data or delete the app and its data using your device settings. Device backups may retain copies according to your backup settings. We cannot access or restore your local progress.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. Email is handled by our email provider. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed for the request or applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence or ask about your information.

For hosting details, see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

The game does not request personal information from players. Please avoid sending personal information about children in support requests.

Changes and contact

If the game’s data practices change, this policy will be updated.
