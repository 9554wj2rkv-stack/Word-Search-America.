Need help or found a problem?

Email northspark21@mail.com. Your email app will open so you can review and send your message.

Tell us what happened, which screen or puzzle you were using, and your device model. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Your progress

Progress is saved locally on this device when storage is available. It does not automatically transfer to another device. Clearing stored data can remove your progress.

Sound and animations

You can turn sound and animations on or off in Settings.

Starting again

Settings → Reset progress lets you start over after confirmation. This cannot be undone.
